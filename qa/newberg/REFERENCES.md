# Newberg / Chehalem reference reconstruction

Reference target: Chehalem Skatepark, Ewing Young Park, 1201 S Blaine Street,
Newberg, Oregon. This is a photo-derived reconstruction, not a surveyed replica.

Primary references:

- https://www.cprdnewberg.org/1498/Chehalem-Skatepark — location, 29,000 sq ft,
  Dreamland construction, capsule/quarter/halfpipe and separate beginner area.
- https://www.dreamlandskateparks.com/oregon — Newberg section: aerial
  `ORnewberg02.jpg` and eight ground-level photos of the actual concrete park.
- https://www.cprdnewberg.org/1346/Ewing-Young-Park — park surroundings/amenities.

The builder's aerial establishes the compact footprint, connected deep end,
west shallow flow pockets, entrance volcano, paired humps and perimeter lawn.
Ground photos establish pale worn concrete, dark rails, orange volcano cap,
low shelter and deciduous/evergreen surroundings. Reference photos are linked;
none are copied into distributed game textures.

The 62 × 46 m working envelope and individual transition dimensions are
estimates from photos, not verified measurements. Bowl contours and connecting
lines still require comparison to surveyed plans or additional aerial detail
before the game can be described as an exact replica. No false exactness claim
appears in the game.
